# mcp-sync Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The `mcp-sync` community is committed to a respectful, welcoming, and
harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- discuss ideas and observable behavior rather than attacking people;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and coordinated disclosure boundaries;
- avoid publishing credentials, private configuration, or personal data; and
- accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is unacceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or attention and unwelcome advances;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory conduct;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring anyone to disclose vulnerability, conduct, identity, or contact
  details publicly; or
- retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.

Good-faith disagreement about design, scope, evidence, or project decisions is
not itself misconduct. It must remain focused on the work and follow this
standard.

## Scope

This Code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the
project in a public or private venue.

## Reporting a conduct concern

Do not publish incident details or personal information. Use the
[conduct contact form](https://github.com/EnjoyableWork/mcp-sync/issues/new?template=03-conduct-contact.yml)
to request private follow-up with no details beyond the request itself. A
maintainer will arrange a private route. If the concern involves a project
maintainer, state only that an alternate contact is requested; do not identify
people in the public issue.

A suspected software vulnerability belongs in the separate
[private security-reporting path](https://github.com/EnjoyableWork/mcp-sync/security/advisories/new),
not the conduct form. Do not use either route to publish credentials, private
configuration, or user data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports as privately as the available route permits,
limit information to people needed for a fair response, and avoid retaliation.
They may seek clarification, remove content, issue a private or public warning,
temporarily restrict participation, permanently ban participation, or refer a
credible threat to the relevant service or authority. Responses should be
proportionate to impact, context, recurrence, and the safety of affected people.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.
Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may themselves violate this Code, but an
unsubstantiated good-faith report does not.

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration
through the same private follow-up route. When possible, a maintainer who did
not make the original decision will review the request; the current
single-maintainer limitation may make independent review unavailable, and that
limitation will be stated plainly.
